# Observator Clew | Visual System

## Lockup

Use `OBSERVATOR CLEW` with the approved two line tagline, `Creating is becoming nearly free. Maintaining never did.`, in horizontal and stacked logo applications. The mark is used alone only where the format is too small for text.

## Layout rule

Geometric planes frame the composition. They do not cover copy, wordmarks, or accessibility-critical information.
