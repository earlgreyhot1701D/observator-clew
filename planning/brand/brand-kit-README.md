## Approved hierarchy

The horizontal and stacked logo lockups include `OBSERVATOR CLEW` and the approved tagline: `Creating is becoming nearly free. Maintaining never did.` The same tagline leads campaign artwork.

## Asset QA

All campaign exports have clear type areas. The black ink is limited to typography, outlines, and the small structural bar within the symbol. No opaque ink block intersects product copy or the wordmark.
